This distribution also includes 16 transparent-RGB 704x1024 card-frame PNG
templates from the AstellarTool project under the MIT terms above. They are
shipped in the CardFrames directory and shown by the application as
transparent_card_frame00 through transparent_card_frame19 (for the frame
types present in Master Duel). They are not relabelled as normal solid frames.

The compact card and resource catalogs themselves contain no image or Unity
asset payloads. They contain card metadata/text, resource hashes, Texture2D
names, and category labels derived from installed game data.
